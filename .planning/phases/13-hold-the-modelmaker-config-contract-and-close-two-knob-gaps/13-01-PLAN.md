Pin the contract mmcli must honour with modelmaker: **a key present in a config's `training`
section is never auto-defaulted, so mmcli must omit what it does not intend to pin.**

does so **by accident**, via `_set()` skipping `None` (`mmcli/builder.py:127-131`), and nothing

and AMP on every CUDA host.

stop them.

**Test mmcli's side only.** Do not import modelmaker or assert anything about its behaviour: the
